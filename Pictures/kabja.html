📖 About the Movie

Kabzaa is a 2023 Indian Kannada-language action crime film directed by R. Chandru. The movie stars Upendra in the lead role as Arkeshwara, along with Sudeepa and Shriya Saran in important roles.

The story is set during the period of British rule in India and follows Arkeshwara, a former air force officer whose life changes after a tragic incident involving his family. He becomes involved in the dangerous world of organized crime.

As Arkeshwara rises through the criminal underworld, he becomes one of the most powerful figures in the region. His journey is filled with violence, rivalries, political conflicts, and struggles for control.

The movie focuses on Arkeshwara's transformation from an ordinary man into a powerful gangster. His rise creates enemies and brings him into conflict with several powerful people.

Kabzaa is known for its large-scale action sequences, period setting, intense atmosphere, dramatic presentation and gangster-themed storyline.
