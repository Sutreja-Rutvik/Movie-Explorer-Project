📖 About the Movie

Tanhaji: The Unsung Warrior is a 2020 Indian historical action film directed by Om Raut. The movie stars Ajay Devgn as Tanaji Malusare, a brave warrior and military commander who served under Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.

The story takes place during the struggle to capture Kondhana Fort, which is controlled by the Mughal forces. Tanaji is given the important responsibility of leading the Maratha forces and capturing the strategically important fort.

Tanaji prepares his soldiers for the difficult battle against the powerful Mughal forces led by Udaybhan Singh Rathore. Despite the challenges and dangers, Tanaji remains determined to fight for Swarajya.

The movie focuses on Tanaji's bravery, leadership, sacrifice and loyalty. The battle for Kondhana becomes one of the most important moments of his life and demonstrates his dedication to his people and his king.

Tanhaji: The Unsung Warrior is known for its large-scale battle sequences, historical setting, powerful performances and dramatic storytelling. The film presents the courage and sacrifice of Tanaji Malusare and his contribution to the Maratha struggle.
