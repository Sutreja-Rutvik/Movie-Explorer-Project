📖 About the Movie

Toxic: A Fairy Tale for Grown-Ups is an upcoming Indian Kannada-language action thriller film directed by Geetu Mohandas. The movie stars Yash in the lead role and is presented as a dark and stylish story aimed at mature audiences.

The film explores a world filled with crime, power and dangerous relationships. Its title and promotional material suggest a darker fairy-tale style narrative rather than a traditional fairy tale.

Yash plays the central character, who is connected to a dangerous criminal world. The story is expected to explore his journey through power struggles, violence and complex relationships.

The movie brings together action, drama and thriller elements with Geetu Mohandas's distinctive filmmaking style. The film also features an ensemble cast with several prominent actors.

Toxic is one of the highly anticipated Kannada films and is planned as a large-scale production. The movie is expected to present a visually distinctive and intense cinematic experience.
