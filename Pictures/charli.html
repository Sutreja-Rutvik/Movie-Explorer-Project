📖 About the Movie

777 Charlie is a 2022 Indian Kannada-language adventure comedy drama film directed by Kiranraj K. The movie stars Rakshit Shetty as Dharma, a lonely man whose life changes completely after a Labrador dog named Charlie enters his life.

Dharma lives a quiet and isolated life. He spends most of his time alone and has little connection with the people around him. His routine changes when Charlie, a playful and energetic dog, enters his home.

At first, Dharma does not want to take responsibility for Charlie. However, as they spend more time together, a strong bond develops between the two. Charlie brings happiness, friendship and emotional warmth into Dharma's life.

Their journey takes them across different parts of India. During their travels, Dharma discovers the importance of love, friendship and companionship. Charlie also helps him overcome his painful past and become a happier person.

777 Charlie is known for its emotional storytelling, beautiful locations, human-animal friendship and strong performances. The movie shows how the bond between a person and a dog can completely transform a life.
