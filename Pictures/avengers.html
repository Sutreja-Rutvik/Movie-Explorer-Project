📖 About the Movie

Avengers: Endgame is a 2019 American superhero film produced by Marvel Studios and directed by Anthony Russo and Joe Russo. It is the direct sequel to Avengers: Infinity War and the 22nd film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

After Thanos uses the Infinity Stones to eliminate half of all life in the universe, the remaining Avengers are left struggling with the consequences of their defeat. The heroes must find a way to overcome their loss and bring back those who disappeared.

Five years after the events of Infinity War, Scott Lang returns from the Quantum Realm and gives the Avengers a possible way to change the past. Tony Stark, Steve Rogers, Natasha Romanoff, Thor, Bruce Banner and the other heroes reunite for one final mission.

The Avengers travel through different moments in time to recover the Infinity Stones. Their mission becomes extremely dangerous when their actions create unexpected consequences and bring Thanos back into the conflict.

Avengers: Endgame is known for its emotional story, large-scale action sequences, memorable character moments and its conclusion to a major chapter of the Marvel Cinematic Universe. The movie focuses on themes of sacrifice, friendship, courage and hope.
