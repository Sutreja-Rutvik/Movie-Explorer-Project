📖 About the Movie

12th Fail is a 2023 Indian Hindi-language biographical drama film directed by Vidhu Vinod Chopra. The movie is based on the true story of Manoj Kumar Sharma, who comes from a poor family in Chambal and dreams of becoming a police officer.

Manoj fails his 12th standard examination and faces many difficulties in his life. Instead of giving up, he decides to start again and work hard to achieve his dream of joining the Indian Police Service.

During his journey, Manoj moves to Delhi and begins preparing for the UPSC Civil Services Examination. He struggles with financial problems, studies, failures and difficult living conditions.

Along the way, Manoj meets people who encourage and support him. His relationship with Shraddha Joshi also becomes an important part of his journey and gives him emotional strength.

12th Fail highlights the importance of hard work, honesty, determination and persistence. The movie shows that failure is not the end and that a person can achieve their goals by continuing to work toward them.
