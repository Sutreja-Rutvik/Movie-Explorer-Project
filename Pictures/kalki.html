📖 About the Movie

Kalki 2898 AD is a 2024 Indian Telugu-language science fiction action film directed by Nag Ashwin. The movie stars Prabhas, Amitabh Bachchan, Deepika Padukone and Kamal Haasan in major roles.

The story is set in a dystopian future in the year 2898 AD, where the world has changed dramatically. Humanity is struggling under the control of powerful forces while hope for a better future begins to emerge.

The story connects futuristic science-fiction elements with characters and ideas inspired by Indian mythology. The central prophecy revolves around Kalki, who is believed to be the final avatar of Lord Vishnu.

Bhairava, a powerful bounty hunter, becomes involved in a dangerous mission that connects him with Sumathi, a mysterious pregnant woman carrying an important child. Amitabh Bachchan plays Ashwatthama, an immortal warrior who protects Sumathi.

Kalki 2898 AD combines futuristic technology, mythology, large-scale action and visual effects to create a unique cinematic world. The movie is the first part of a planned two-part story.
