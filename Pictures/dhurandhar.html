📖 About the Movie

Dhurandhar is a 2025 Indian Hindi-language spy action thriller film written and directed by Aditya Dhar. The movie stars Ranveer Singh in the lead role along with Akshaye Khanna, Sanjay Dutt, Arjun Rampal and R. Madhavan.

The story follows an undercover Indian intelligence operative who enters the dangerous criminal world of Karachi. He works from within the underworld while secretly carrying out a high-stakes mission.

The protagonist gradually rises through the criminal network and becomes deeply involved in its power struggles. While maintaining his secret identity, he attempts to uncover and dismantle a dangerous network from inside.

The movie combines espionage, crime, political conflict and intense action. The story draws loose inspiration from several real geopolitical events while presenting its own fictional narrative.

Dhurandhar is known for its large-scale action sequences, intense performances, detailed world-building and long runtime. The film is the first part of a two-film series, followed by Dhurandhar: The Revenge.
