📖 About the Movie

Inception is a 2010 science-fiction action thriller film written and directed by Christopher Nolan. The movie stars Leonardo DiCaprio as Dom Cobb, a skilled extractor who enters people's dreams to steal valuable information.

Cobb is given a difficult task that is completely different from his usual missions. Instead of stealing an idea from someone's mind, he must plant an idea inside the mind of another person. This process is known as "inception."

To complete the mission, Cobb assembles a team of specialists, including Arthur, Ariadne, Eames and Yusuf. Together, they enter several layers of dreams where time, reality and imagination become increasingly difficult to distinguish.

As the team moves deeper into the dream world, they face dangerous obstacles and unexpected consequences. Cobb also struggles with memories from his past that threaten the success of the entire mission.

Inception is widely known for its complex storytelling, creative dream sequences, impressive visual effects and exploration of ideas about dreams, memory and reality.
