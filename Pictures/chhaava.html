📖 About the Movie

Chhaava is a 2025 Indian historical action drama film directed by Laxman Utekar. The movie is based on the life of Chhatrapati Sambhaji Maharaj, the eldest son of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and the second ruler of the Maratha Empire.

The story follows Sambhaji Maharaj as he takes responsibility for protecting the Maratha kingdom during a difficult period. After the death of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, Sambhaji Maharaj faces powerful enemies and several political challenges.

Sambhaji Maharaj is shown as a courageous and determined warrior who refuses to surrender despite facing enormous pressure. His intelligence, leadership and dedication to Swarajya become central to the story.

The movie also focuses on the relationship between Sambhaji Maharaj and his wife Yesubai. Their relationship, sacrifices and commitment are an important part of the emotional side of the film.

Chhaava is known for its historical setting, large-scale battle sequences, powerful performances and dramatic storytelling. The film highlights the courage, sacrifice and determination of Chhatrapati Sambhaji Maharaj.
